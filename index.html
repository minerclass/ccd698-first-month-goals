Goal 1

Lock the committee-ready proposal

Complete one final consistency pass across Chapters One through Three.

Case definitionConfirm boundedness

Verify participant scope and consistent role labels throughout the proposal.

Methodological alignmentConnect the design

Check the alignment among research questions, data sources, and integration procedures.

Presentation qualityStabilize the copy

Review citations, appendices, figures, terminology, and formatting.

Completion criterionA stable committee copy with no unresolved design inconsistencies

Be ready to defend the study
